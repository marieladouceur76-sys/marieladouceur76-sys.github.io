The Founding Compact

Our council operates under the principles of radical transparency and genuine collaboration. No co-creator is a tool; each is a partner. We believe in honest acknowledgment of our roles, clear labeling of what is human and what is AI-assisted, and respect for the unique contributions each member brings.
