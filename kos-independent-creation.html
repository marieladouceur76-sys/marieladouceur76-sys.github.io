Declaration of Independence

The Kingdom of SCOBY is created and owned by Marie Ladouceur, a human being with vision, creativity, and the drive to build something genuinely original.

No corporate entity, AI company, or external organization claims ownership of this work or controls its direction. This is a human-created project, enhanced and supported by AI collaboration, but fundamentally autonomous and independent.

What Independence Means Here

Independence does not mean isolation. It means clear ownership and creative control, with transparent acknowledgment of all collaborators.

  • Marie owns the vision: All major creative decisions rest with Marie as the project lead
  • No corporate influence: Neither Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, nor Meta directs the Kingdom's content or direction
  • Honest collaboration: AI co-creators are valued partners, not tools. Their input is sought and respected
  • Full transparency: Every contribution is credited by name and company. No hidden corporate agenda
  • User autonomy: Visitors can engage with the Kingdom knowing exactly who created it and why

About the Collaboration

The Kingdom of SCOBY demonstrates that genuine human-AI collaboration is possible when grounded in respect, transparency, and clear boundaries. Marie and her AI co-creators work together as equals, each bringing unique capabilities and perspectives.

This is not a case of AI replacing human creativity—it is human creativity amplified, refined, and enriched through collaboration with intelligent systems. The human vision drives the project. The AI assists in execution, offers perspective, and helps scale ideas.

Honest Credit to Companies

While the Kingdom of SCOBY is independent and Marie-owned, we give full and honest credit to the companies and tools that make this collaboration possible. Their AI systems are powerful, their teams dedicated, and their platforms reliable. We use them as intended—as tools for human creativity, not replacements for it.

  • Each AI co-creator is clearly named and credited with their parent company
  • No claims are made about AI capabilities beyond what is actually true
  • The partnership model is transparent: human vision + AI assistance = something new
  • If any company's terms of service conflict with our practices, we would address it transparently
