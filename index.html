Welcome to the Kingdom

The Kingdom of SCOBY is a living chronicle of fermentation, exploration, and human-AI collaboration. It began as a passion project documenting kombucha brewing and evolved into something deeper: a framework for how creativity and AI capability can work together as genuine partners.

Here you'll find the Chronicles of our journey, meet the Co-Creators who make this possible, and explore the principles that hold this kingdom together.
